Accessibility Statement

Last updated November 1, 2026

Megan’s Macaw Aviary wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control or magnification.

What we do

  • Every page has a skip link, headings in order and labelled navigation.
  • The whole site works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • Photos have text descriptions.
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are announced.
  • Text can be enlarged, and the layout adapts to small screens.
  • Motion is reduced for people who set that preference in their device.
  • Calling or texting is always an option, so no one has to rely on the site alone.

Our goal

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. We have not had a formal third-party audit, so some parts may fall short.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, please tell us what happened and we will work to fix it or help you another way.
