Cookie Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device.

Our use of cookies

Megan’s Macaw Aviary does not set cookies. We also do not use local storage, advertising trackers, analytics tags or social media widgets on this site. Because of this, we do not show a cookie banner.

Fonts

The fonts on this site are served from our own website, not from Google or another outside service.

Outside links

If you follow a link to another site, such as Google Maps, Google reviews, Instagram or Facebook, that site may set its own cookies. Please read its policies.

Changes

If we add anything that uses cookies or similar technology, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. See also our Privacy Policy.
