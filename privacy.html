Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Megan’s Macaw Aviary (“we”, “us”) handles when you use this website, and what you can ask us to do with it. We are based in Raleigh, North Carolina.

What we collect

This website does not have accounts, payments or a server-side contact form. The message form on the contact page is built in your browser. Nothing you type there is sent to this website. You choose whether to send it from your own text or email app, or to copy it.

If you call, text or email us, we receive the information you share, such as your name, phone number or email address, and what you write. If you adopt a bird, we also keep the details needed for the adoption, such as your contact details and the bird’s records.

Automatic data

Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes technical data when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems. We do not run analytics or advertising tools on this site.

Why we use information

  • To answer your questions and arrange visits.
  • To complete adoptions and keep records for the birds we place.
  • To keep the website secure and working.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site (such as our hosting provider), with your own phone carrier or email provider when you contact us, and when the law requires it. Links to Google Maps and Google reviews open on Google’s sites, where Google’s own privacy policy applies.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies. See the Cookie Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as needed to answer you. Adoption records are kept as long as needed for the bird’s records and for legal and tax purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Some adoption records may need to be kept by law. We will reply as promptly as we can, and within any time limit the law sets. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

You can call or text Megan at +1 805-259-3980 or use the contact page.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and uses security headers to reduce risks. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above. If we ever add analytics, cookies or a form that sends data to us, we will update this policy first.
